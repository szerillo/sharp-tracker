# Market making on Kalshi and Novig: what the makers earn (11/10)

Sean: "look into opportunities where we serve as a market maker at good prices on Kalshi and Novig to pick up daily profits and compound."

## Method (sharp/maker_test.py)
Every taker trade has a maker on the other side. For each pregame Kalshi and Novig trade on a moneyline, spread or total (Oct 31 to
Nov 10; NBA, NFL, NHL, CFB, CBB, MMA, soccer totals), the maker's side and price are scored against Pinnacle's no-vig price at the same
line: at the fill, 30 minutes later, and at the close (last quote in the hour before the start). 1.84 million trades, 1.08 million scored
(the rest are at lines Pinnacle did not quote). All figures are cents per contract, weighted by contracts; z is across games.
- Fees: Kalshi charges resting orders 0.0175 x price x (1 - price) per contract on its NFL, NBA, NHL and college football games (0.44c at
  50c). Applied to every Kalshi row here. Novig charges nothing pregame.
- Novig's tape gives the resting order's outcome and price (checked against its quotes: 86% of fills in markets 1c+ wide print below the
  mid of that outcome).
- Grading checked per game: Kalshi moneyline prices of 33% / 50% / 67% won 33% / 50% / 67%.

| Kalshi | 820,641 | $5.5M | +0.58c | +0.36c | +0.29c (z 9.4) | **-0.09c** | -$8,800 |
| Novig | 259,931 | $3.8M | +0.25c | +0.17c | +0.13c (z 7.4) | **+0.13c** | +$8,100 |

Makers buy a little under Pinnacle's fair price and give about half of it back by the close. On Kalshi the maker fee takes the rest:
quoting everything passively there is a small loser. On Novig the whole maker side earns about 0.2% of the money it puts up.

## 2. A resting bid under Pinnacle's fair price (the bettable version)
A bid k cents under Pinnacle's fair on every side, repriced at each Pinnacle reading (5 to 10 minutes apart), filled by every taker trade
at or through it, paid at our own bid. This is the whole pool: it assumes we are first in line for every fill.

| | Fills | Risked per day | At the close | After fee | Whole pool, $ per day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kalshi, 1c under | 246,303 | $1.42M | +0.84c (z 16) | **+0.45c** | $12,600 |
| Kalshi, 2c under | 42,922 | $288k | +0.86c | +0.48c | $2,900 |
| Kalshi, 3c under | 5,729 | $69k | -1.99c | -2.38c | -$2,900 |
| Novig, 1c under | 46,444 | $554k | +0.32c (z 10) | **+0.32c** | $3,200 |
| Novig, 2c under | 9,985 | $151k | +0.33c | +0.33c | $900 |
| Novig, 3c under | 3,153 | $56k | +0.44c | +0.44c | $400 |

A fill 1c under fair keeps about half a cent. Asking for more does not pay more: a taker who sells 3c under Pinnacle usually knows
something, and on Kalshi those fills lose 2c by the close.

From every maker fill (section 1 population):
- **Size of the fill.** Fills of $10k+ are the informed ones: -0.35c on Kalshi before fee, -0.48c on Novig. Fills under $100 are the best
  (+0.59c and +0.50c).
- **Price.** Makers who end up holding the underdog (under 25c) earn +1.72c on Kalshi and +1.64c on Novig; makers holding a 75c+ favourite
  lose 0.76c and 1.14c. Takers overpay to buy favourites and sell longshots.

## 4. The risk: this is not a daily profit
- **The edge is about 1% of the money risked and the positions are not hedged.** A maker ends up holding whatever takers sold, mostly
  underdogs. Over these 11 days favourites won more than priced in the biggest games (75c+ sides: priced 89%, won 93%), so Kalshi's
  makers lost 5.1% of $57M at settlement while beating the close; the 1c-under bid lost 17% of $14.9M. Novig's makers won 5.0%. Neither
  number says anything about the edge; both say the day-to-day swing is many times the expected profit.
- **Queue.** The pool figures assume every fill. At 1c under fair we usually join a bid that is already there; our share would be a
  fraction and the back of the queue gets the worse fills.
- **Speed.** Our Pinnacle reading is 5 to 10 minutes old. The 3c-under and 24h+ rows are what being slow costs. The firms quoting these
  books reprice in seconds.
- **Spreads and totals** are scored only where Pinnacle still quotes the line at the close (moneylines always are), so those rows are
  a little flattering.
- **Scale.** Capturing 2% of the 1c-under flow on both venues would be about $40,000 a day at risk for about $300 a day expected.

## Read
- There is a real, measurable maker edge, and Novig is the better venue (no fee, the maker side is profitable as a whole). On Kalshi the
  maker fee leaves only selective quoting.
- The shape of a quoting rule the data supports: bid 1c under Pinnacle's fair, last hour before the start, spreads and totals first,
  small size per order, pull the quote when Pinnacle moves, nothing more than a day out on Kalshi, no NBA moneylines on Novig.
- It is a volume business with a thin edge and directional risk, not a compounding daily profit. It needs orders placed and repriced by
  API on Sean's own keys. Next step before any money: a paper quoter in the fast job that logs the bid it would rest each cycle and
  marks fills from the trade tape with the queue ahead of it (Novig's quotes carry size at the best price), so share of fills and
  out-of-sample CLV are measured.
