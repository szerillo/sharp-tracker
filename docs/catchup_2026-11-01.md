84 Pinnacle-origin moves (2+ points devigged inside 15 minutes) on the 5-minute tape, 10/30 to 11/1. Scripts: sharp/moves.py (who
followed and when), sharp/catchup.py (what the exchanges actually offered); data/research/catchup.json.

## Was the lag buyable?

The exchange mid lags because one side of the book is stale, not because the price you can take is. Paying the ask right after the
Pinnacle move:

| Venue | Entry | n | vs Pinnacle's new price | Positive | CLV |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kalshi (fee incl.) | first tick | 29 | -0.1 pts | 7% | -0.4 |
| Kalshi | +15 / +30 / +60 min | 25-28 | -0.1 to -0.4 | 4-8% | -0.5 to -0.7 |
| Novig | first tick | 38 | -0.3 | 42% | -0.7 |
| Novig | +30 / +60 min | 33-36 | +0.7 | 36-39% | +0.1 |

Soft books still on the old number when the move is detected (FanDuel / Caesars / DraftKings, within 45 minutes): +1.0 pt CLV on 28
cases. Following at Pinnacle itself: -0.2 (43).

## What this says

1. Kalshi's ask is already at Pinnacle's new price when we see the move: the "46-minute lag" is a stale bid, not a stale offer. No
   window there.
2. Novig is cheaper than Pinnacle's new price about 40% of the time, but on average it is not, and the CLV is negative early and flat
   later. Thin (about 35 moves); no window yet.
3. The only positive cell is a soft book still on the old number (+1 pt CLV, 28 cases). The Today page already marks these ("old
   price still at FD / Caesars / DK"). Worth taking when you can get size; too thin to call an edge.
4. Our 5-minute polling is not the bottleneck for the exchanges (their offer moves with Pinnacle); for the soft books it might be (they
   are polled every 20 minutes). Re-run at 2-3 weeks (about 400 Pinnacle moves).
