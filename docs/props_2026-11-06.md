# Player props, first pass (11/6/2026)

Script `sharp/props_test.py`, results `data/research/props_<sport>.json`. One snapshot per game 10 minutes before the start
(2023-26, every book), graded on ESPN box scores (`data/boxscores`). Players without a box line are void.

## Standing lean
Pinnacle's unders lose only the vig (NBA -4.3%, NHL -4.2%) and overs lose about double (NBA -8.9%); unders are the better side but not
a bet at Pinnacle's price by themselves.

## Where US books misprice props (book price 2%+ better than Pinnacle's no-vig fair at the same line)
- **NBA 3-pointer unders: 2453-2198, +7.9%, every season positive (+20.3, +5.5, +7.0, +7.0)**, and at every big book (BetRivers +9.0,
  DK +8.7, FD +8.1, Bovada +9.1, Caesars +7.0). The books price the threes overs for the public and leave the under long.
- NBA points unders +2.5%; all NBA unders with a price gap +1.9% (4 of 4 seasons); every NBA price-gap over loses.
- **NHL goalie-saves unders: 99-54, +24.9%, 4 of 4 seasons** (small). NHL shots-on-goal unders 1098-1075, +5.3% (3 of 4 seasons).
- NHL assists with a 4%+ gap +17.3% (4 of 4 seasons), mostly long-priced overs at DK.
- NFL props: nothing that holds (samples per market are small; interceptions and passing TDs are noisy).
- Line gaps (book line half a point to a point off Pinnacle's) lose 5% in every NBA market.

## Live
`sharp/props_live.py` runs in the 20-minute signals job: the Odds API current odds (the backtest's source, about 1 credit a game) for
games inside 3 hours; FD, Caesars or DK pricing an NBA threes / NHL saves / NHL shots under 2%+ better than Pinnacle's no-vig is logged
as a paper lean (Paper rules on the board), graded on the box score. Paper until it has a forward record; limits on props are low.
