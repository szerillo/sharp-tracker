# NFL player props and the 60-minute prop window (11/8)

Three new downloads from The Odds API tonight, all one snapshot per game, graded on ESPN box scores:
- NFL player props, 12 markets, 10 minutes before kickoff, 2023 to 11/8/2026: 1,220 games (the saved list had stopped at last February's Super Bowl; 135 games from this season added).
- NBA threes, 60 minutes before tip, 2023-26: 4,207 games.
- NHL goalie saves and shots on goal, 60 minutes before puck drop, 2023-26: 4,536 games.

Flat 1 unit per bet at the book's price. Pushes void. "Seasons +" counts seasons with a positive return.

## 1. NFL props: almost nothing at 10 minutes before kickoff

Every market, both sides, four tests (Pinnacle's own price; a book 2%+ better than Pinnacle's no-vig at the same line; a book's line off Pinnacle's; off the main line). Of about 80 buckets, these are the only ones worth a line:

The 2%+ price-gap rule that works for NBA threes does nothing in the NFL: the US books hold these lines well, and most of the time the gap is Pinnacle being off, not the book.

### The one candidate: the rushing-yards outlier book

Pinnacle hangs one rushing-yards line for a player, a book hangs one line 4.5+ yards away. Bet the side the gap favours at that book (its line is lower: over; higher: under).

| Cut | Bets | Record | ROI | By season |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All, every book quote counted | 272 | 168-104 | +16.3% | +20 / +4 / +14 / +54 |
| One bet per player and game | 169 | 99-70 | +10.1% | +17 / -2 / +4 / +47 |
| 2+ other books within a yard of Pinnacle (the book is the outlier) | 121 | 85-36 | +31.8% | +23 / +6 / +45 / +86 |
| 0-1 other books near Pinnacle (Pinnacle is the outlier) | 151 | 83-68 | +3.8% | -6 / -4 / +3 / +26 |
| Outlier book, one bet per player and game | 100 | 65-35 | +21.9% | +18 / +3 / +27 / +86 |
| Same, Pinnacle's quote newer than the book's | 129 | 84-45 | +22.5% | 4/4 |
| Neighbour: book 2.5-4 yards off, outlier, deduped | 777 | 426-351 | +3.5% | -4 / +4 / +8 / +10 |

The edge is the outlier-book case, which is the stale-book mechanism again (Pinnacle moved, two or more books followed, one did not). Both sides win (overs 26-12, unders 39-23). Books: BetOnline 53-32, FD 38-27, DK 27-18, Caesars 12-4, BetRivers 25-19.

Why this is paper and not a tier: 100 deduped bets (z +2.5 against the -110 breakeven), it is one bucket out of about 80 looked at, and the edge disappears a yard away (2.5-4 yards is flat). By Sean's convention that is at most tier C after a forward record. Live now in sharp/props_live.py as "outlier book": Pinnacle one line, FD / Caesars / DK one line 4.5+ yards away, 2+ of BetRivers / Bovada / BetOnline / the other two bettable books within a yard of Pinnacle, inside an hour of kickoff. Paper, both sides.

## 2. NBA threes unders at 60 minutes before tip

The live feed moved to a 1-hour window on 11/7; the rules were backtested at 10 minutes. Re-run at T-60 (1,680 under bets at 2%+ against Pinnacle's proportional no-vig):

| Filter | T-10 (11/7 doc) | T-60 (tonight) |
|---|---|---|
| 2%+ | 2,012 bets, +7.8%, 4/4 | 1,680 bets, +7.6%, 4/4 |
| 3%+ | 1,274, +8.8%, 4/4 | 1,035, +8.9%, 3/4 |
| 4%+ | 791, +11.6%, 4/4 | 612, +11.2%, 4/4 |
| Power-devig EV > 0 | | 1,667, +7.7%, 4/4 |
| Book's own no-vig 4+ pts below Pinnacle's (the live filter since 11/7) | 1,655, +9.8%, 4/4 | 1,364, +6.3%, 3/4 |
| Book's own no-vig only 2-4 pts below | 357, -1.7%, 2/4 | 316, +13.3%, 4/4 |
| Pinnacle quote 5-20 min old | 33, -13.3% | 22, +12.0% (small) |

The edge is still there an hour out and at about the same size. But the 4-point fair-gap filter added on 11/7 flips at T-60: the 2-4 point bets it drops were the best group tonight. The filter was fit to the 10-minute snapshot. What holds at both windows is a plain EV floor: 4%+ against Pinnacle's no-vig is +11.6% and +11.2%, every season positive, both times. Recommendation: replace the 4-point gap filter with EV 4%+ (strict fair). Not changed yet.

## 5. Three hours before kickoff (11/9; 1,220 games, anytime TD and rushing yards, 19,330 credits)

Same rules, snapshot 3 hours out instead of 10 minutes.

|---|---|---|

| same, EV 2-5% / 5-10% / 10%+ | +10.7% / +14.4% / +2.5% | +11.4% / +7.6% / -3.5% |
| Neighbour: fair 15-25% | | -8.2% |
| Rushing-yards outlier book, gap 4.5-7, deduped | 100 bets, 65-35, +21.9%, 4/4 | 81 bets, 48-33, +9.9%, 2/4, z +1.2 |
| Rushing-yards gap 4.5+, Pinnacle the odd one | +3.8% | -14.5% |

Both hold their sign three hours out, and both are weaker. The anytime TD band still clears the vig at every EV below 10% (the 10%+
slice is the stale-number tail, as at T-10). The rushing-yards outlier loses most of its edge: inactives come out about 90 minutes before
kickoff, and that news is what the stale book has not priced. Three hours out the book is just disagreeing. Keep both inside the
1-hour live window; nothing to bet earlier.

1 credit per game per extra run. Not changed; Sean's call.
