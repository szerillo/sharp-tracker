# Pushback: when it arrives, what it costs, and when to lock a bet in (11/9)

Sean: "we want to figure out when we're getting pushback on these bets, so we know when it's safe to lock them in. If we're going to get
negative order flow against us eventually, the bet ends up not being plus EV."

"Pushback" = the signals the board treats as opposition: a Kalshi 4+ point move, bet back, big money, sharp move, Polymarket top-200
accounts, or a sized sharp flag or system bet on the other side. Paper rules never count.

- Most pushback is there before we flag, and the hold rule catches it.
- When it comes later: a median of 8.1 hours after the flag and 4.1 hours before the start (quartiles 1.7 / 10.1 hours before).
- Of bets clean at a given time, the share that got pushback later: 25% at T-24h, 21% at T-6h, 11% at T-3h, 4% at T-1h, 3% at T-30m.
  Sharp flags draw it more often (43% at T-6h, 9% at T-1h), almost all from other Polymarket accounts.
- What the later pushback was matters more than when it came:

| Polymarket sharp accounts on the other side | 38 | 23-15 | +11.1% | +0.58 |

found the same for sharp accounts on 2,750 opposed flags: following the first sharp was +4.1% opposed later vs +3.5% never opposed.

- A late Kalshi move against a side costs it about 20-30% ROI, at every horizon.
- The value of waiting (the last column minus "bet all now", which includes the worse price on the sides the market moves toward):
  about +1.6 points of ROI from T-24h, +1.2 from T-6h, +0.8 from T-3h, +0.6 from T-1h, nothing inside 30 minutes.
- Late moves are spread out: of the sides clean at T-6h and moved later, 63 came 3-6h out, 56 at 1-3h, 63 at 15-60 minutes, 31 in the
  last 15 minutes.
- By sport, clean at T-3h: NBA 10.7% and MLS 13.7% get moved later (lineup and team news); NFL 4.3%; NHL 1.6% and CFB 1.4% almost never.

## What to do with it
- **Inside 3 hours: lock it in.** About 5% of bets get a late Kalshi move after that, and waiting is worth under a point.
- **Flagged 6+ hours out:** waiting until about an hour before the start and placing it only if no Kalshi move against has appeared is
  worth about 1-1.5 points of ROI. Worth doing when the price is not running away. In NBA, take the later read seriously (news).
- **A Polymarket sharp taking the other side after we are in is not pushback that matters.** Do not wait for it or get out for it.
- Nothing changed on the board. The hold rule (any signal against at the time you would bet = hold) stays as it is.

## When to place system bets (open vs close, from sharp/rule_replay.py on Pinnacle 2020-26; data/research/rule_replay.json)
Systems are judged on results, not CLV (Sean, 11/9). For timing, though, the open-to-close move is the right measure: it is the same
bet at two prices. Positive = the price moves toward the rule's side, so bet early.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| nhl_early_home_fav_qual | 397 | 258-139, +9.4% | +0.63 pts | 7 of 7 |
| nhl_pl_fav_gap | 205 | 79-126, +1.5% | +1.37 pts | 7 of 7 |
| nhl_pl_fav_strong_opp | 358 | 136-222, -0.1% | +1.12 pts | 7 of 7 |
| nhl_pl_dog_bigml | 64 | 40-24, +11.6% | -0.43 pts | 3 of 7 |
| cfb_road_dog_low_total (conference) | 255 | 132-113, +4.4% | -0.41 pts (the dog's number grew in 41%) | |
| cfb_road_streak_dog | 434 | 214-210, -2.0% | -0.35 pts | |
| nba / cbb unders that need the total to drop | | | n/a: the rule fires on the drop, so it is a close bet | |

Read: the NHL favourite rules get worse as the day goes on (the market agrees with them): bet them early. The CFB road-dog rules get
better: wait. The wind rules, curve leans and exchange rules have no open-to-close history to replay here; the tracker's 'best price
later' fields cover them going forward.
