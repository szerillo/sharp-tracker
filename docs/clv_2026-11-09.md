# Closing line value: what we have and what was added (11/9)

## Added
- **Props** (sharp/props_live.py): in the last signals run before the start (inside 12 minutes), one Pinnacle-only call per game for the
  flagged markets. Each lean stores Pinnacle's closing line, its closing no-vig probability for our side at our line, CLV = that minus the
  price paid (points of probability), how far Pinnacle's fair moved toward us since the flag, and line value (points Pinnacle's close moved
  past its flag-time line toward our side; the only read for the outlier-book leans, which bet a number Pinnacle does not hang).
- **Curve leans** (sharp/curve_live.py): the same for CBB first-half totals, CFB first-quarter and team totals, NHL first-period lines and
  third-period totals. These markets are not on the tick tape, so the bet tracker had no close for them (including the tier A / B rows).
- Both show on the board next to the tracker record ("beat Pinnacle's close on X of N, average +Y pts"). About 1 credit per market per game.

Game-line bets already had CLV in the bet tracker (sharp/bet_log.py, Pinnacle's no-vig close from the tape): about 1,500 bets.

## Do the "signal against" holds cost us? No.

Kalshi move and bet back lose to the close as bets, which raised the question of whether a Kalshi move against one of our bets means the
market is about to move our way (so the hold would be costing us). It does not: the bets those signals oppose are worse still.

hold). Small samples per tag; re-read at the weekly re-check.
