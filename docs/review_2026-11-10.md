The same direction in all nine slices. The gap for all rows is about 2 standard errors, and this is a split found by looking, so it is a
lead and not a rule. But it lines up with four earlier results: chasing exchange steam is the worst entry and the bet-back side won
(11/1); exchange moves Pinnacle did not follow lose 1.2 points (checkpoint); makers earn and takers pay (docs/maker_2026-11-10.md); and
the two momentum rules above. What our signals flag is often the top of a move. When the price keeps going our way we were late; when
it comes back we get the side at a better number and it wins more.

## 3. What I would change
1. **Rest the bet, do not take it.** For every flag, the board shows the price to rest (Pinnacle fair minus 1c on Novig or Kalshi) next
   to the take price, and the log records both outcomes: filled or not, and the result. On the log so far a bid 1c under fair filled on
   29% of bets, and those were the winners. This costs nothing to paper and tests section 2 forward. Pre-registered here: filled-at-rest
   against never-filled, graded at 150 filled.
2. **Sharp flags to paper size until they beat Pinnacle's close.** They have not in 150. Keep logging, keep the hold rule (a flag against
   a system bet still holds it: system bets with sharps against are 14-11, with sharps on side 10-13, so it carries no information
   either way yet).
3. **Stop adding rules that follow a move.** Anything new that fires because a price has moved is tested as a fade or a wait first.
4. **Leans fire 53 hours out and pay 2.3 points over fair.** The price was better later on 66 of 117 by 3.0 points. Leans should be
   rested or delayed by default (the pushback doc already says wait when 6+ hours out).

## 4. What was missed in the data and needs a re-test
- **Odds API, systems at the price we get.** Every system was graded at Pinnacle's close. Live, we enter 15 to 50 hours early at a book or
  exchange price 0.7 to 2.3 points worse than fair. Re-grade each live rule at the Odds API snapshot nearest its usual fire time, at
  the best US price, and separately at the close. If a rule only wins at the close it should be bet late; if it only wins early it is a
  line-movement rule. No credits (data in hand).
- **Odds API, the open.** The 11/5 replay found the side the market moves toward wins at its opening price in every sport (+6 to +22%)
  and is gone by the close. Nobody has tested what predicts that side at the open: which book moves first, opener disagreement between
  Pinnacle and the US books, limits. That is the one large effect in the whole replay and it is untested as a signal. No credits.
- **Sharp data, the yardstick.** The live list ranks wallets by CLV against Polymarket's own close. 11/5 kept that method because it
  beat the Pinnacle ranking in history, but live it is 0.06. Re-run the walk-forward on the last 60 days only, both rankings, with
  Pinnacle's close as the score and the wallet's fill time as the entry. If neither holds on recent data the list is not a signal.
- **Sharp data, the other side of the trade.** We follow wallets that take prices. The maker test says the edge is on the resting side.
  Polymarket's tape names the maker of every fill: rank wallets by the CLV of their resting fills and see who the consistent
  liquidity providers are and which side they lean. Not done for any venue.
- **Exchange moves Pinnacle does not follow** (-1.22 on 1,044, z -10.7): the fade, with the entry at the moment we can tell Pinnacle has
  not followed. Listed 11/10, not yet run.

## 5. Other places to look
- **Soft markets as a maker** (docs/maker_2026-11-10.md, sharp/offers_live.py, live on the board 11/10): Novig team totals and props,
  where the book is 5 to 20c wide. The trade tape for those markets started 11/10; fill rates in about a week.
- **ProphetX** as a third exchange for the same scan, once Sean adds its key as a secret.
- **Tie pricing on Kalshi's period moneylines** (quarter, half, period): a tie loses there and is refunded at Pinnacle, so they need a
  tie probability to price. If Kalshi's are priced off the two-way number they are off by the tie rate. Testable from the Odds API
  period lines and results.
- **Scheduled news**: NBA injury reports post at fixed times; measure each venue's lag after one.
